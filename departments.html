Our structure

Departments & areas of work.

An overview of directorate areas and their general responsibilities.

Working together

The Ministry’s directorates work together to support effective administration, digital technology, innovation, research, financial management and the delivery of its programmes.

01

General Administration

Official correspondence, records management, meeting coordination, protocol, logistics and administrative support.

02

Digital Technology

Digital literacy, ICT skills, practical technology learning and responsible technology adoption.

03

Innovations

Creative problem-solving, innovation initiatives and support for youth-led ideas.

04

PPBME

Planning, programme coordination, monitoring and evaluation, and performance reporting.

05

Research, Statistics & Information Management (RSIM)

Research, information management, statistics and evidence-informed planning.

06

Human Resource Management (HRM)

People management, staff coordination, development and human-resource administration.

07

Finance

Financial planning, budgeting, expenditure documentation and financial reporting.

08

Internal Audit

Internal controls, compliance review, risk awareness and accountability support.

09

Public Relations

Public information, media relations, ministry announcements and communication with stakeholders.
