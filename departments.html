How to read this section

The items below describe the kinds of work and public records associated with each directorate. They are not a claim that every activity has already taken place. Completed activities will be reported in the newsroom or publications library when verified and cleared for release.

General Administration

Coordination of official correspondence, meetings, protocol, records and logistical support.

Public record examples: approved notices, meeting summaries and administrative circulars.

Digital Technology

Digital literacy, ICT skills, practical technology learning and responsible technology adoption.

Public record examples: digital skills resources, workshop notices and learning materials.

Innovations

Creative problem-solving, innovation initiatives and support for youth-led ideas.

Public record examples: challenge briefs, idea showcases and project summaries.

PPBME

Planning, programme coordination, monitoring and evaluation, and performance reporting.

Public record examples: approved work plans, programme frameworks and evaluation summaries.

Research, Statistics & Information Management

Research, information management, statistics and evidence-informed planning.

Public record examples: research briefs, data summaries and information resources.

Human Resource Management

People management, staff coordination, development and human-resource administration.

Public record examples: public role notices, training summaries and organisational information.

Finance

Financial planning, budgeting, expenditure documentation and financial reporting.

Public record examples: approved budget summaries and public financial accountability reports.

Internal Audit

Internal controls, compliance review, risk awareness and accountability support.

Public record examples: public governance statements and approved audit-related summaries, where disclosure is appropriate.

Public Relations

Public information, media relations, ministry announcements and communication with stakeholders.

Public record examples: press releases, media statements, public notices and campaign materials.

For published activity records, visit the institutional activity archive and publications library.
