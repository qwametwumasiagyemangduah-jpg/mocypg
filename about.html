Who we are

About the Ministry.

A Youth Parliament Ghana ministry focused on communications, digital technology and innovation.

Our purpose

Helping young people participate confidently in a digital world.

The Ministry’s public-facing work centers on digital literacy, responsible technology use, communication and youth innovation. It provides a space to share ministry priorities, leadership information, programmes and approved public resources.

This website presents information about the Ministry within Youth Parliament Ghana. It is not the website of Ghana’s national ministry with a similar name.

01 / MISSION

Promote capability.

Promote responsible technology use, digital literacy, communication and innovation among young people.

02 / VISION

Connect opportunity.

A connected and innovative youth community equipped to participate confidently in the digital world.

03 / APPROACH

Serve with purpose.

Support youth participation through information, collaboration and practical learning opportunities.
