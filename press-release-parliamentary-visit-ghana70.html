Official press release · Youth Parliament of Ghana

Parliamentary visit, inauguration & Ghana@70.

An update from the national leadership following the parliamentary visit on 11 November 2026.

PRESS RELEASE

Issued: 13 November 2026 · Youth Parliament of Ghana

TO: ALL MEMBERS OF THE YOUTH PARLIAMENT OF GHANA

SUBJECT: UPDATE ON PARLIAMENTARY VISIT, INAUGURATION AND PROPOSED GHANA@70 CELEBRATION

The leadership of the Youth Parliament of Ghana wishes to express its sincere appreciation to all members who participated in our parliamentary visit to the Parliament of Ghana on Wednesday, 11th November 2026.

We are pleased to announce that our delegation received recognition from the Parliament of Ghana for the visit, which provided our members with an opportunity to observe parliamentary proceedings, understand the operations of the institution and gain practical knowledge of Ghana’s democratic governance.

It was indeed a successful and memorable engagement. We appreciate everyone who made time to participate, and we extend our best wishes to members who, due to unavoidable circumstances, could not attend.

UPDATE ON OUR PROPOSED 27TH NOVEMBER LAUNCH

During our discussions, concerns were raised regarding the proposed 27th November 2026 launching and inauguration date. Several members have academic commitments, including examinations, graduations and project defences. Additionally, the month of November comes with several national activities, including preparations towards Farmers’ Day celebrations.

After careful consideration and consultations among the leadership, we have proposed a new direction: to integrate the official launching and swearing-in ceremony of the Youth Parliament of Ghana into our Ghana@70 National and International Youth Leadership Week celebrations in February 2027.

This proposal has received positive consideration from several national stakeholders, who recognise the opportunity it presents to celebrate Ghana’s 70 years of independence while showcasing youth leadership, democratic participation and national development.

The full programme outline, implementation arrangements, activities and other relevant details will be communicated to all members in due course.

NEXT STEPS

The national leadership will hold another strategic meeting on Saturday, 14th November 2026, to deliberate on the proposed arrangements and determine the next steps towards implementation.

We urge all members to remain calm, committed and united throughout this process. We understand the expectations surrounding this institution, and we are determined to get things right.

The Youth Parliament of Ghana is growing, and expectations are high. We must therefore demonstrate maturity, discipline, effective leadership and a strong sense of responsibility.

Please be assured that every important development and decision will be communicated through our official communication channels.

Thank you for your patience, understanding and continued commitment.

Together, let us build a stronger Youth Parliament and contribute meaningfully to Ghana’s development.

Long live the Youth Parliament of Ghana!

Long live Ghana!

HON. OSMAN ABDUL LATIF CHEBOO
President & Founder
Youth Parliament of Ghana
