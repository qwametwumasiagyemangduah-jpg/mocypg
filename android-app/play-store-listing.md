## Short description (under 80 characters)

## Full description
Stay connected with the Ministry of Communications, Digital Technology and Innovations within Youth Parliament Ghana.

The Ministry of Communications YPG app brings key ministry information together in one convenient place.

FEATURES
• News and announcements: Read ministry updates and public notices.
• Leadership: Learn about the ministry’s leadership and directorates.
• Programmes: Explore activities and initiatives.
• Publications: Access public information and available resources.
• Contact information: Find ways to get in touch with the ministry.
• Convenient access: Browse the ministry website in an app-style experience, with support for offline access to previously cached pages.

The app is designed to make ministry information easier to find on mobile devices.

Website: https://mocypg.vercel.app/
